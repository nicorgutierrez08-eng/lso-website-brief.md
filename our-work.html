Our Work

Hands-on impact across the boroughs.

See how our volunteers are making a hands-on impact across the boroughs.

St. Clement's Food Pantry assets/img/event-st-clements.jpg

St. Clement's Food Pantry

We volunteer here on an ongoing basis distributing meals to those in need, working to make pantry operations cleaner and more efficient while helping community members through the food registration process.

Weekly, every Saturday

New Sanctuary Coalition assets/img/event-new-sanctuary.jpg

New Sanctuary Coalition

We support asylum seekers and immigrants navigating their new environment, offering real-time translation and assisting with community needs and administrative tasks.

Weekly, every Wednesday

JASA multilingual event assets/img/event-jasa.jpg

JASA

We connect with the elderly community through multilingual events in English, Spanish, and Chinese, including AI and tech literacy classes, Easter and Lunar New Year celebrations, and quality time together.

St. Peter's Church drive assets/img/event-st-peters.jpg

St. Peter's Church

We organized a community outreach drive, collecting and distributing food, toys, and clothing to local individuals and families.

MOCA cultural event assets/img/event-moca.jpg

MOCA

We partnered with the Museum of Chinese in America for a cultural event teaching Lunar New Year traditions, with hands-on craft sessions and a multilingual storytime.

MintBox planting kits assets/img/event-mintbox.jpg

MintBox

We collaborated to distribute free home-planting kits to underserved communities, helping families grow sustainable food at home.

Visit mintboxny.com →

KidsKnowRights childcare assets/img/event-kidsknowrights.jpg

KidsKnowRights

We team up on joint community initiatives, including organizing and leading the childcare program for families at the New Sanctuary Coalition.

More to come from all of these incredible organizations.
