Join the Team

Use your languages to give back to NYC.

We are always looking for passionate high schoolers in NYC who speak English, Spanish, or Chinese. If you want to use your language skills to give back to the community, here is how to start.

Three steps

From hello to your first event.

Volunteers at a club meeting assets/img/get-involved.jpg

  1. 1

    Get in touch

    Send any of us an email. A quick hello is all it takes to get started.

  2. 2

    Attend a club meeting

    Come to our next meeting at school to learn about current partnerships and upcoming initiatives.

  3. 3

    Start volunteering

    Join our current initiatives or lead your own.
